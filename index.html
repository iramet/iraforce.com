Why IraForce

Manual security operations don't scale. IraForce does.

Paper timesheets, radio check-ins, and spreadsheet rosters break down the moment you add another site. IraForce replaces every disconnected tool with one real-time system of record.

The old way

  • No visibility into patrols or checkpoints missed

Features

Everything your security operation needs, in one place

Ten integrated modules built specifically for security workforce management.

Smart Scheduling

AI-assisted shift planning & deployment

Build and publish shift rosters in minutes with AI-assisted planning, automated guard deployment, and one-click open-shift filling. Supervisor assignment and configurable clock-in windows keep every shift accountable from the first minute.

Live GPS Tracking & Geofencing

Real-time location on a live map

See every guard’s live location on a real-time map, monitor patrol routes as they happen, and get instant alerts the moment a geofence boundary is breached — no more blind spots across your sites.

Patrol Management

Checkpoint tours with proof of presence

Define patrol routes with QR, NFC, or GPS checkpoints, verify every tour was actually walked, and get automatically alerted on missed checkpoints so nothing falls through the cracks.

Incident Reporting

Digital, evidence-backed, escalatable

Guards log incidents digitally with photo and geo-tagged evidence in seconds. Configurable escalation workflows route the right incidents to the right people, instantly.

Attendance & Payroll

Geo/biometric clock-in to paycheck

Geo- and biometric-verified clock-in feeds automated timesheets and approvals. Payroll and charge-rate sync means hours worked flow straight into what clients are billed and guards are paid.

Panic / SOS Alerts

One tap to the SOC, instant dispatch

A single tap sends a guard’s emergency alert straight to the SOC with live location attached, triggering instant dispatch and a full audit trail of the response.

Curated Questions

Answers before you have to ask

How fast can we go live?

Most single-site teams are scheduling and clocking in through IraForce within a week — import your roster, set geofences and checkpoints, and publish your first shift.

Does the mobile app work without signal?

Yes. Patrol check-ins and clock events queue on-device and sync automatically the moment connectivity returns — nothing is lost mid-shift.

Can clients see reporting in real time?

Every Growth and Enterprise plan includes a branded client portal with live patrol proof, incidents, and attendance — no phone calls required.

Is pricing per guard or per site?

Per active guard, per month. There is no charge for admin seats, supervisors, or client-portal users.

Do you support SSO and advanced RBAC?

Enterprise plans include SAML/OIDC SSO, granular role-based access control, and a dedicated customer success manager.

What happens if a checkpoint is missed?

Supervisors get an automatic real-time alert the moment a scheduled checkpoint is skipped, with the guard, site, and time attached.
