The Platform

One dashboard. Every guard, site, and shift in real time.

IraForce runs on two connected surfaces: a real-time admin portal for supervisors and ops leaders, and a native mobile app for the guards and supervisors in the field.

app.iraforce.com

Real-time operations dashboard — KPIs, live map, and activity feed at a glance

Incident Reporting

Every incident, tracked from report to resolution

Guards log incidents in the field with photo and geo-tagged evidence. Supervisors see every open, in-progress, and escalated incident in one queue — no more digging through email threads or paper logs.

Workforce & Digital ID

A single source of truth for your entire guard force

Roles, certifications, site assignments, and digital ID cards live in one roster — synced in real time with scheduling, attendance, and payroll, so nobody is ever scheduled for a site they aren't certified or cleared for.

Patrol Management

Proof that every checkpoint was actually walked

Define patrol routes with QR, NFC, or GPS checkpoints. Verify tours were completed on schedule, and get alerted automatically the moment a checkpoint is missed.

Guard Mobile App

Built for the field, not the office

A native iOS & Android app puts everything a guard needs in their pocket: shift schedules, patrol checkpoints, digital ID, incident reporting, and secure team messaging with supervisors and dispatch.

  • Live schedule & next-shift alerts
  • QR / NFC / GPS checkpoint scanning
  • Photo-backed incident reports
  • One-tap panic / SOS with live location
  • Secure messaging with supervisors & dispatch
