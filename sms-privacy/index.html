Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

IraForce ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our security personnel, clients, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us during onboarding, dispatch coordination, or through our website (https://iraforce.com/). This may include:

  • Full name and contact details (mobile phone number, email address)
  • Shift schedules, clock-in/clock-out logs, and post assignment details
  • Call records and SMS communication logs related to operational and support workflows

2. How We Use Your Information

IraForce uses the collected information strictly for legitimate operational and service purposes, including:

  • Sending upcoming shift notifications, clock-in reminders, and post instructions via voice calls and SMS
  • Coordinating day-to-day operational queries and field check-ins with security guards
  • Providing account, scheduling, and dispatch support
  • Maintaining compliance, security, and accurate workforce records

3. SMS Communications & Mobile Data Privacy

When you opt in to receive text messages from IraForce, we use your mobile phone number solely to deliver operational alerts, shift updates, clock-in reminders, and support messages connected with our phone call services.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your mobile opt-in consent and phone number are never shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing use.

4. Your Choices & Opt-Out Rights

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages from IraForce at any time by replyingSTOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us through https://iraforce.com/.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure. We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill operational, legal, and compliance obligations.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact IraForce at https://iraforce.com/.
